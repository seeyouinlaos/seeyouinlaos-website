The quiet capital on the Mekong: a French avenue that ends in a triumphal arch, the golden stupa of Pha That Luang, and a riverfront that fills every evening. It is where we get married.

03 · China

Kunming

In Kunming, late spring arrives in colour: in April and May the streets are lined with 蓝花楹 (jacaranda), a canopy of soft violet that moves through the city. Our early-March days come before it — the season of the first cherry blossom in the temple courtyards. Often called the “Spring City” for its year-round climate, Kunming keeps its gentle rhythm in every season.
