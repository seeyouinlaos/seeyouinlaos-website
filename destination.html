Bangkok

The shared city days that open the journey — the river at golden hour, the temples of the old town, and the night train waiting at Krung Thep Aphiwat to go north. The journey returns here for its last two nights.

02 · Laos

Vientiane

The quiet capital on the Mekong: a French avenue that ends in a triumphal arch, the golden stupa of Pha That Luang, and a riverfront that fills every evening. This is where the whole journey leans — the wedding day is here.

03 · China

Kunming

In Kunming, late spring arrives in colour. Streets are lined with 蓝花楹 (jacaranda), forming a canopy of soft violet that moves through the city. Often called the “Spring City” for its year-round climate, Kunming takes on a different rhythm during this season — everyday routes, cafés, and neighbourhoods briefly framed by bloom.
