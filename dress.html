Sunday, 28 February 2027 · Morning

Lao Traditional Dress · Blue

Temple Ceremony · 08:00 – 12:00 · Wat Ong Teu, Vientiane

The Buddhist morning is dressed in Lao tradition, in blue. Shoulders and knees are covered inside the temple, and shoes come off at the door. A sinh may be borrowed or made in Vientiane — Guest Relations helps anyone who would like that arranged before the morning.

Six references · Lao traditional dress

Sunday, 28 February 2027 · Afternoon and evening

Black Tie

Coffee & Cake · Vow Ceremony · Wedding Dinner · Souphattra Heritage, Vientiane

From the return out of the temple, the day is black tie and stays black tie until the end of dinner. The courtyard is warm and the evening is long: a dinner jacket, a long dress, and shoes that survive a garden are the whole of it.

Six references · black tie

21 February – 08 March 2027

Resort Wear

The travelling days · Bangkok · the night train · Vientiane · Kunming · Lijiang

Everything outside the wedding day is resort wear — easy, unhurried, and warm enough for a Lijiang evening at 2,400 metres. Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else on this list.
