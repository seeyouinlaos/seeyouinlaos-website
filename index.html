First light

The morning
opens the day.

The wedding day begins with the Sacred Morning Ritual — the Lao alms giving, Tak Bat. At dawn, monks walk silently in line with their alms bowls while those taking part kneel along the route and place sticky rice and small food into the bowls. It is a living religious tradition, not a staged attraction — a quiet daily bond between the community and the monastic order.

For guests who wish to make an offering themselves, an individual offering is prepared in advance and reserved in the Guest Area. In Lao Buddhist tradition the offering is a personal act of giving and merit-making — it comes from the person who presents it rather than from the hosts; the personal contribution is part of the meaning of the ritual itself.

The wedding day then continues at Wat Ong Teu Temple in Vientiane: a Buddhist morning ceremony from nine until around midday, unhurried and full of meaning. Afterwards we return together to the hotel for coffee & cake before the vows.
