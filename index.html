Good to know.

Your stay

Souphattra Vientiane Hotel, in Vientiane. Arranged for you as part of the wedding block; room details to follow.

Getting there

The route: Bangkok to Nong Khai, then across the border to Vientiane. Full travel details to follow, so please wait for our green light before booking.

Weather in late February

dry season

Warm days and cooler mornings by the river. Bring a light layer for the dawn alms giving.

Visa & currency

Visa-on-arrival for most nationalities; our team meets you at immigration and guides you through. Currency: Lao Kip (LAK); US dollars widely accepted.

Your contacts

Extend your journey

How to say yes.

  1. Tell us you're in

    One line is enough. Write to guest.relation.seeyouinlaos@gmail.com, or message us on LINE (ID seeyouinlaos). This is your address for every question until you land in Laos.

  2. Wait for the green light

    We confirm the hotel first; then you book your own flight for the best price and flexibility. You'll hear from us the moment it's safe to book.

  3. Send us your flight number

    Reply to the same email with your booking number. That locks in your room, your transfers, and your seat at every table.

  4. Enjoy the ride

    From the moment you land, you're our guest: vans, rooms, dinners and every transition are taken care of.
