The Dress Guide

What to wear,
day by day.

Four moments, four looks. Warm days, cooler evenings by the river; every dress note below is tied to its moment of the weekend.

01Arrival & Stay
Elegant Resort Wear

Linen, silk, soft colour. Breathable and unhurried, made for warm afternoons in the courtyard.

  • WomenFlowing dresses, elegant separates, sandals
  • MenLinen shirt, chinos or tailored shorts, loafers
  • ColourIvory, sand, soft botanicals
  • ComfortVientiane is warm; choose fabric over structure
02Alms Giving · at dawn
Lao Traditional Dress

The morning ritual asks for covered shoulders and a long skirt. Sinh and silk scarves can be arranged in Vientiane if you would rather not travel with them.

  • WomenSinh (Lao skirt) with a covered-shoulder blouse and pha biang scarf
  • MenLong trousers with a shirt; a silk scarf if you have one
  • RespectShoulders and knees covered throughout the ritual
  • FootwearSlip-off shoes; you will be barefoot at moments
03Vow Ceremony
Black Tie

The most formal hour of the weekend, in the courtyard as the day softens.

  • WomenFloor-length gown or refined formal dress
  • MenTuxedo, black bow tie, patent shoes
  • NoteNo full white — reserved for the bride
  • GroundGarden setting; a block heel travels better than a stiletto
04Wedding Dinner
Black Tie

From sunset drinks beside the pool into the courtyard dinner, and late into the night.

  • WomenEvening gown or elegant cocktail-length formal
  • MenTuxedo; a velvet or ivory dinner jacket is welcome
  • EveningA light wrap for the river air after dark
  • NoteNo full white — reserved for the bride

Before You Travel

Good to know.

Your stay

Souphattra Heritage Vientiane. One shared home for the whole celebration, arranged for you; room details to follow.

Getting there

The route: Bangkok to Nong Khai, then across the border to Vientiane. Full travel details to follow, so please wait for our green light before booking.

Weather in late February

dry season

Warm days and cooler mornings by the river. Bring a light layer for the dawn alms giving.

Visa & currency

Visa-on-arrival for most nationalities; our team meets you at immigration and guides you through. Currency: Lao Kip (LAK); US dollars widely accepted.

Your contacts

Before you travel, write to guest.relation.seeyouinlaos@gmail.com or message us on LINE or WhatsApp — scan the codes in your Guest Area. Your Guest Relations team looks after you personally, from arrival to the last transfer.

Extend your journey

Warmly encouraged. Some guests continue onward from Vientiane to Kunming and Lijiang, flying home from Lijiang via Bangkok on 6 March. Our guest relations team is happy to help you plan; just write to us.
