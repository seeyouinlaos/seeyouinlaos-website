# SEE YOU IN LAOS — Luxury System (BINDING SYNTHESIS)

Aman at Sea = PRIMARY (structure, product intelligence, hospitality system).
Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection = SECONDARY (editorial drama, visual restraint, photographic presentation).
See You In Laos = the identity, the wedding, the actual journey, the booking logic.
For every problem: one decision, one rule. No hybrid mess. The result must read only as **see you in laos.**

Format per area: REQUIREMENT → AMAN → RITZ → BEST FOR SYL → WHY → **FINAL RULE**.

---

## A. GLOBAL NAVIGATION
- REQUIREMENT: orient a guest across one journey, three products, five destinations, plus a private area.
- AMAN: worlds (Vessel / Destinations / Voyages / Experiences) + two persistent CTAs (Plan your voyage / Request a call).
- RITZ: minimal campaign nav; everything funnels to one proposition.
- BEST: Aman.
- WHY: our complexity needs worlds; Ritz's flattening loses the product.
- **FINAL RULE**: Public nav = The Journey at a Glance · Before · The Wedding · After · Good to Know · Your Invitation. Exactly two persistent CTAs sitewide: `Join the Journey` (→ Guest Area) and `Contact Guest Relations` (human channel). No third CTA ever.

## B. INVITATION ENTRY
- AMAN: "Plan Your Voyage" wizard, curation-framed. RITZ: "Request a Quote".
- BEST: Aman framing + SYL's own invitation ritual (plaque box) which neither brand has.
- **FINAL RULE**: Entry stays the invitation box ritual; the landing line follows Aman's grammar: "Your journey, prepared around you." Never "register", never "book now" at the door.

## C. HERO
- AMAN: season framing, sentence-case serif. RITZ: full-bleed photo, one serif line, kicker, CTA pair immediately visible.
- BEST: Ritz composition, SYL words, Aman case.
- **FINAL RULE**: Full-bleed owner photograph · kicker `HARUTHAI & SUTHEP · SUNDAY, 28 FEBRUARY 2027 · VIENTIANE, LAOS` in micro-caps · one serif line ("see you in laos." / "Travel first. Wedding second. Memory always.") in sentence case · CTA pair `Join the Journey` + `Discover the Journey` directly in the hero.

## D. JOURNEY OVERVIEW
- AMAN: season → regions → featured voyages, one region at a time. RITZ: one glance composition, image-dominant.
- BEST: current SYL "Journey at a Glance" (already owner-fixed) refined with Ritz image treatment.
- **FINAL RULE**: Map + four destination captures + THREE chapter rows (Before/Wedding/After) remain the single overview. Captions move INSIDE the capture images, uppercase micro-labels (Ritz). No fourth competing overview may ever be added.

## E. ROUTE / MAP
- AMAN: route map opens every voyage. RITZ: none (destination slider only).
- BEST: Aman. **FINAL RULE**: The geographic three-country map (Thailand/Laos/China; Bangkok→Nong Khai→Vientiane→Kunming→Lijiang) is the first visual of the Journey overview and may never be replaced by a single-destination photograph.

## F. JOURNEY PRODUCT COLLECTION (our 3 selectable products)
- AMAN: voyages as route-named cards with fact line + `View details` + quote CTA.
- RITZ: itineraries flattened into campaign slides.
- BEST: Aman. **FINAL RULE**: Before the Wedding / The Wedding / After the Wedding are presented as three journey products, each with a fact line (`21 – 27 FEB · Thailand → Laos`), one editorial sentence, and one open action. The Wedding always carries the strongest visual weight (cherry accent, full-bleed moment).

## G. JOURNEY DETAIL · DAY-BY-DAY
- AMAN: Day N → place H2 → one paragraph → experience preview → quiet enquire line.
- RITZ: no day system.
- BEST: Aman. **FINAL RULE**: Inside every chapter the unit is the DAY/MOMENT block: `TIME/DATE (micro-caps, cherry)` → serif title → venue line → ONE story paragraph → optional VIEW control. Nothing about a day renders twice on the same surface.

## H. DATES · DURATION · START/END
- **FINAL RULE** (Aman grammar): `7 MAY 2027 – 13 MAY 2027 · 6 nights, 7 ports` becomes ours: `24 FEB 2027 · 20:25 → 25 FEB · 06:45 · 1 night · Bangkok → Nong Khai`. Dates exact, words soft, one line, dot-separated.

## I. DESTINATION MODULES
- AMAN: region romance pages, zero operational data. RITZ: destination slider with in-image captions.
- BEST: merged — Aman's separation, Ritz's presentation. **FINAL RULE**: Destinations never carry prices or booking state on public surfaces; destination photography uses in-image uppercase captions; operational data lives only in Guest Area steps.

## J. EXPERIENCE DISCOVERY + DETAIL
- AMAN: category-led (Culinary Journeys / Cultural Insights…), individual experiences only as day previews.
- RITZ: horizontal rails, numbered pagination, VIEW.
- BEST: SYL hybrid decided: rails per destination (owner-fixed) carrying Aman category labels, Ritz pagination.
- **FINAL RULE**: Place rails stay inside chapters; each chip = image · name · category caps · `View experience`; detail = contained overlay (gallery → story → facts → maps); close returns to position; rails show `01 / N` numbered position, no arrows. Experiences remain informational — never booking products.

## K. ACCOMMODATION OVERVIEW + ROOM DETAIL
- AMAN: category cards with one 2–3-line paragraph (size · differentiator · location) + ONE shared "All Suites Include" grid.
- RITZ: cabin drama, image-first.
- BEST: Aman structure with Ritz-scale lead image.
- **FINAL RULE** (Guest Area step 02): each room = large image · name · key-fact grid (sqm / bed / occupancy / garden or balcony) · `More details` (full dossier) · contribution · `Book this room`. Amenity lists move to ONE shared "Every room includes" layer; per-card copy max three lines. Public site shows only the stay editorial + Explore the Stay panel.

## L. IMAGES (ratio · captions · carousels · rails)
- **FINAL RULE**: Photography leads (Ritz); 4:3 in rails, full-bleed for chapter moments; captions in-image, 9–10px caps, ivory; carousels use invisible tap zones + dots (existing owner rule) and numbered `01 / N` counters; typography-tile fallback whenever no approved image exists (008 rule) — a wrong image is worse than no image.

## M. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY
- AMAN: sentence-case Lyon serif ~31px, tiny caps eyebrows, 14px body. RITZ: 61px caps serif.
- BEST: Aman case + Ritz scale contrast.
- **FINAL RULE**: Eyebrows: 10–11px caps, .2em tracking (cherry when the wedding). Headlines: PP Editorial Old serif, sentence case, clamp(26–42px); caps reserved for micro-labels and in-image captions only. Body: 14–15px/1.6 sans, max-width ~560px. Metadata: 10–11px caps, dot-separated.

## N. FACT PRESENTATION
- **FINAL RULE** (Aman): facts are one-breath lines or small dl-grids — never prose sentences repeating numbers. Metric first. `31 sq.m. · King bed · 2 adults + 1 child · Garden`.

## O. CTA HIERARCHY
- AMAN: two persistent, verbs of curiosity. RITZ: one proposition repeated.
- **FINAL RULE**: Sitewide pair `Join the Journey` / `Contact Guest Relations`. Inside chapters only `View …` / `Explore …`. In booking only `Book this stay/room/journey` · `BOOKED` · `Remove` (frozen owner CTAs). One repeated editorial CTA band ("See you in Laos?") between chapters — the only Ritz band we adopt, once per surface.

## P. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE
- **FINAL RULE** (owner + Aman): SUMMARY → SELECT → OPEN → INTERACT → APPLY → CLOSE → RETURN, one open layer at a time on every surface; dossiers always behind `More details`; second paragraph always behind a click.

## Q. EDITORIAL PAUSES · QUOTE / HOST MOMENTS
- RITZ: press quote + dark transitions. BEST: Ritz mechanics, SYL voice.
- **FINAL RULE**: The hosts are our only quoted voice. One italic serif host line per surface maximum ("Travel first. Wedding second. Memory always."). One dark full-bleed transition — into The Wedding chapter — nowhere else.

## R. INCLUSIONS · HOSTED · STATUS · CONTRIBUTION
- **FINAL RULE** (frozen status model kept, Aman softness): HOSTED / BOOKED / YOUR CHOICE / COMPLIMENTARY / INCLUDED as micro-cap chips; hosted language stays factual-generous: "the second wedding night · hosted by Haruthai & Suthep"; contributions always `USD n per guest` as metadata, never in narrative sentences; TOTAL CONTRIBUTION is the single persistent money surface (sticky bar + plan), computed only by the one calculation layer.

## S. PERSONAL SELECTION & CURRENT STATE
- **FINAL RULE** (AIDA/owner + Aman curation): every decision row shows its current value while collapsed (`Overnight Train · 2 guests · USD 150 · BOOKED`); per-person choices list the guests by preferred name; nothing requires reopening to be understood.

## T. MOBILE
- **FINAL RULE**: Safe area → header → content (one shell, both surfaces); map first, then captures; rails swipe with snap; hamburger-free Guest Area (numbered stepper is the navigation); no floating controls over imagery; 0px horizontal overflow is the acceptance bar (Aman itself ships 15px — we do not).

## U. FOOTER / END STATE
- **FINAL RULE**: Public ends with Your Invitation (07) then the minimal footer (names · date · contact). Guest Area ends with 05 · Your Plan → Review & Send. No deep link farms.

# FINAL SEE YOU IN LAOS LANGUAGE SYSTEM

- **EYEBROW STYLE**: 10–11px caps, dot-separated, numbered where architectural (`04 · The Wedding`); cherry only for wedding surfaces.
- **HEADLINE STYLE**: serif, sentence case, first-person-plural warmth, never uppercase: "The journey at a glance." / "The morning opens the day."
- **JOURNEY NAMING**: chapters are the products: Before the Wedding · The Wedding · After the Wedding. Legs named as routes (Aman): "Bangkok → Nong Khai → Vientiane".
- **DESTINATION LANGUAGE**: place-first sensory openers, triads, no logistics: "Gardens, spring light and, with luck, the first cherry blossom."
- **ITINERARY LANGUAGE**: exact time + soft verb: "20:25 · Bangkok slips away; the Mekong arrives with the morning."
- **EXPERIENCE LANGUAGE**: name · category caps · one-line teaser ending in an image, not a claim; detail paragraphs sensory, facts separated.
- **STAY LANGUAGE**: design-story first (Aman ryokan pattern): rooms "around one quiet courtyard"; size/bed/occupancy as fact grid; "breakfast every morning" as fact, not perk.
- **WEDDING MOMENT LANGUAGE**: TIME → MOMENT → VENUE → one sentence of meaning → dress as quiet metadata: "05:00 · Alms Giving · Souphattra Heritage Vientiane · Lao Traditional Dress."
- **HOSTED LANGUAGE**: "hosted by Haruthai & Suthep" — stated once, factually; never "free", never apologetic.
- **INCLUDED LANGUAGE**: "Breakfast included" / "van pickup & luggage service · included" — the word `included` closes the line.
- **CONTRIBUTION LANGUAGE**: "Your contribution · USD 65 per guest / night" — possessive, metadata position; the cultural meaning precedes the amount (Sacred Morning offering pattern).
- **STATUS LANGUAGE**: micro-cap chips only: HOSTED · BOOKED · YOUR CHOICE · COMPLIMENTARY · INCLUDED · JOINING · OFFERING RESERVED. Never REQUESTED on journey surfaces (frozen rule).
- **CTA LANGUAGE**: verbs of invitation — Join · View · Explore · Discover · Book (booking only) · Remove. Never Submit/Buy/Ticket/Register.
- **MICROCOPY**: one clause, present tense, reassurance through fact: "Choosing a destination sets nothing in stone and costs nothing."
- **BODY COPY**: max ~3 lines before an image or a disclosure; second paragraph always behind More details.
- **EDITORIAL INTRO**: one sentence that carries the whole surface: "Four chapters carry the journey… Open a chapter — everything else stays quietly out of the way."
- **IMAGE CAPTION**: in-image, caps, tiny, place-only: `VIENTIANE · THE WEDDING`.
- **DATE / DURATION**: `21 FEB – 06 MAR 2027 · Thailand → Laos → China`; times 24h; ranges with en-dash; nights counted explicitly (`3 nights`); every product carries exactly one fact line.

**Binding note:** this document is the design specification for the clean rebuild. Where it conflicts with older aesthetic notes, this document wins; where it conflicts with frozen business rules (prices, statuses, NO CRUISE, QR/image rules, booking CTAs), the frozen rules win.
