# Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection — Editorial Reference (SECONDARY)

Rendered-page research, 05 Sep 2026, desktop 1366px, campaign page "Journey Unlike the Rest" (ritzcarltonyachtcollection.com/campaigns/journey-unlike-the-rest). Full-page render inspected (6,630px) + computed typography.

## 1. Page anatomy (top → bottom)
1. **HERO**: full-bleed photograph, dark soften, tiny letter-spaced kicker line, one serif line in uppercase — "UNLIKE THE REST" — and directly beneath it TWO CTAs: `REQUEST A QUOTE` + `DISCOVER THE DETAILS`. Nothing else competes.
2. **"DISTINCT — IN EVERY WAY"**: an image, then a benefit trio in three narrow text columns (Customer Confidence / Effortless Experience / Exceptional Service class of content), minimal words each.
3. **Editorial quotation**: an italic serif quote with a press attribution (Condé Nast Traveler) — a trust pause, not a feature.
4. **CTA BAND**: "FOR A JOURNEY WORTHY OF YOU" + one button. This exact band REPEATS later — one conversion path, offered rhythmically.
5. **"DISCOVER CAPTIVATING DESTINATIONS"**: horizontal destination slider (ALASKA · ASIA · CARIBBEAN · SOUTHERN EUROPE · NORTHERN EUROPE · SOUTH PACIFIC) with **numbered pagination 1–5**, full-bleed photography, uppercase captions set INSIDE the image.
6. **"ABOARD"**: three quiet cards (Unmatched Wellness · Seamless Connection to the Sea · World-Class Dining) — image, small heading, two lines.
7. **Dark yacht transition**: italic serif "Our Yachts" over full-bleed night imagery, then LUMINARA / ILMA / EVRIMA as sub-chapters. A deliberate mood shift into the product.
8. Deep utility footer.

## 2. Measured typography
- Headings: Adobe Caslon Pro serif, 61px, **UPPERCASE**, near-zero letterspacing (0.38px) — drama through scale + case, not tracking.
- Body: neutral Helvetica/Arial 16px.
- Labels/kickers: very small, strongly letter-spaced caps.
- Serif carries emotion in caps; sans carries function in silence.

## 3. Editorial principles observed
- **Image dominance**: photography is the layout; text floats sparsely upon or beneath it.
- **Extreme copy restraint**: headline + at most two short lines before the next visual.
- **Controlled CTA repetition**: the same single proposition band recurs instead of many different CTAs.
- **Numbered pagination** (1 2 3 4 5) instead of arrows — calm, oriented, no chrome.
- **Captions inside imagery**: destination names live in the photograph in small caps.
- **Editorial pauses**: quote blocks and dark full-bleed transitions reset the breath between functional sections.
- **Trust via third party**: one press voice, once.

## 4. What See You In Laos takes from Ritz (candidates — finalized in the synthesis doc)
1. Hero = photograph + one serif line + the CTA pair immediately (primary + discover).
2. One repeated CTA band between chapters (ours: "See you in Laos?" → Join the Journey).
3. Destination captions set inside the images, uppercase, tiny.
4. Numbered pagination (01/04) on rails.
5. Italic host-voice quote as the editorial pause ("Travel first. Wedding second. Memory always.").
6. Dark full-bleed transition into the Wedding chapter (our LUMINARA moment is Vientiane).

## 5. What we deliberately do NOT take
- Uppercase 61px headlines everywhere (Aman's sentence-case serif is closer to our identity; caps reserved for micro-labels).
- The funnel shallowness: Ritz flattens product depth into a campaign — our journey needs Aman's day/level structure underneath.
- Cruise vocabulary of any kind (standing NO CRUISE rule).
