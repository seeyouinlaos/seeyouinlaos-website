# Aman at Sea — Luxury Hospitality Language (PRIMARY language reference)

Verbatim material captured from rendered pages, 05 Sep 2026, with the rules it demonstrates.

## 1. Naming
- The vessel has a meaning, stated immediately: "Amangati, Sanskrit for 'peaceful motion', presents a matchless experience on the water."
- Voyages are named as ROUTES ("Palma de Mallorca to Nice") with an optional narrative overname in small caps ("BALEARIC ADVENTURE") and occasionally a themed title ("Atlantic Passage: A Voyage of Renewal").
- Experience categories are two-word poetry: Culinary Journeys · Cultural Insights · Wellness Rituals · Scenic Discoveries · Coastal Interludes · Active Adventures.

## 2. Metadata grammar
- `7 May 2027 – 13 May 2027 · 6 nights, 7 ports` — full dates, then duration, then scope. Numbers do the precision; words stay soft.
- Suites: "354 square metres (3,810 square feet)" — metric first, imperial in brackets; "Located aft on Deck 5." Location is one closing clause.

## 3. Sentence patterns (verbatim)
- Place-first openers: "Bold brushstrokes of Mediterranean blue extend from the picturesque Balearic Isles…"
- Sensory inventory: "Emerald islands unfold in a sequence of rainforest reserves, volcanic peaks and castaway beaches…"
- The personal promise: "Your voyage, designed around you." / "…we will curate a personalised voyage."
- The disclosure invite: "Enhance your voyage with optional Shore Experiences. Enquire to learn about additional experiences offered."
- Curation credentials without bragging: "A private audience with a Tuscan count at his family estate. A pizzaiolo's secrets, shared over hand-stretched dough in Sorrento."
- Wellness cadence: "Unhurried and deeply personal, each is designed to nurture wellbeing while reflecting the character of its destination."

## 4. Verb & CTA vocabulary
Discover · Explore · Enquire · Request · Plan · Curate · Unfold · Reveal · Await.
CTAs observed: `Plan your voyage` · `Request a call` · `Enquire now` · `View details` · `Discover more` · `Request E-Brochure` · `View all voyages` · `Sign Up`.
Never: Buy, Book now (in narrative surfaces), Ticket, Deal, Offer.

## 5. Tone rules distilled
1. Sentence case headlines; caps only for tiny eyebrows.
2. One paragraph per idea; the second paragraph goes behind a click.
3. Price never touches poetry — amounts appear only in decision/quote contexts.
4. Every list of three: "breathwork and sound bathing to botanical rituals" — triads everywhere.
5. The guest is addressed as a curious traveller, never as a buyer.
6. Time is soft ("as the day softens", "unhurried days") but data is exact (dates, sqm, decks).
