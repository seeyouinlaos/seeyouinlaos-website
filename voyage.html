Part of the morning · Tak Bat / Sai Bat

Morning Alms-Giving

A traditional morning offering of food to Buddhist monks — a personal act of generosity, and one of the oldest ways a day begins in Laos. Every guest who comes to the temple takes part; there is nothing to arrange and nothing to pay.

Sangkhathan Temple Offering

A personal offering of useful everyday items, prepared for presentation to the monks as part of the Buddhist tradition of giving. It is separate from the morning alms-giving of food.

We will prepare the offering for you. In keeping with the personal meaning of the ritual, each participating guest contributes to and presents their own offering personally.
