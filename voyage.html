Part of the morning · Tak Bat / Sai Bat

Morning Alms-Giving

Guests attending the Temple Ceremony are invited to take part in the traditional morning alms-giving, Tak Bat. This is the moment in which food is respectfully offered to Buddhist monks — one of the oldest ways a day begins in Laos. It is self-pay: each guest arranges their own small offering of food on the morning itself. No amount is set here.

Sangkhathan · Optional · USD 15 per guest

Sangkhathan Temple Offering

A personal offering of useful everyday items for the monastic community, presented by you. It is not the morning alms-giving: Tak Bat is the food offered during the ceremony, arranged and paid for by each guest (self-pay), while the Sangkhathan is a separate, optional offering that you may choose to have prepared in your name.

We will prepare the offering for you. In keeping with the personal meaning of the ritual, each participating guest contributes to and presents their own offering personally.
