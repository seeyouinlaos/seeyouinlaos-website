Part of the morning · Tak Bat / Sai Bat

Morning Alms-Giving

Guests attending the Temple Ceremony are invited to take part in the traditional morning alms-giving, Tak Bat. This is the moment in which food is respectfully offered to Buddhist monks — one of the oldest ways a day begins in Laos. There is nothing to arrange and nothing to pay.

Part of the Temple Ceremony · no charge

The morning alms-giving · Tak Bat

Optional · USD 15 per guest

Sangkhathan Temple Offering

A personal offering of useful everyday items for the monastic community, presented by you. It is not the morning alms-giving: Tak Bat is the food offered during the ceremony and carries no charge, while the Sangkhathan is a separate, optional offering that you may choose to have prepared in your name.

We will prepare the offering for you. In keeping with the personal meaning of the ritual, each participating guest contributes to and presents their own offering personally.
