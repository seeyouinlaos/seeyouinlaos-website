The evening before the wedding, together at a Vietnamese house in Vientiane: phở, bánh mì and coffee, under red lattice, neon and warm light.

Cảm Ơn cooks the way its founders’ grandmother did in Vietnam. The beef broth simmers for more than twelve hours, the bánh mì bread is baked in the house every morning, and the coffee comes from family farms in the volcanic soil of Paksong, dripped through the Vietnamese phin.
