Dress code

The wedding day carries two codes, and the travelling days carry a third. The photographs are references, not rules: they show the register, not a uniform. Nothing is bought or hired through this website.

Sunday, 28 February · Morning

Blue Lao Traditional Dress

Temple Ceremony · 08:00 – 12:00 · Wat Ong Teu, Vientiane

The Buddhist morning is dressed in Lao tradition, in blue. Shoulders and knees are covered inside the temple, and shoes come off at the door. A sinh may be borrowed or made in Vientiane — Guest Relations helps anyone who would like that arranged.

Sunday, 28 February · Afternoon and evening

Black Tie

Coffee & Cake · Vow Ceremony · Wedding Dinner · Souphattra Heritage

From the return out of the temple, the day is black tie and stays black tie until the end of dinner. The courtyard is warm and the evening is long: a dinner jacket, a long dress, and shoes that survive a garden are the whole of it.

21 February – 08 March

Resort Wear

The travelling days · Bangkok · the night train · Vientiane · Kunming · Lijiang

Everything outside the wedding day is resort wear — easy, unhurried, and warm enough for a Lijiang evening at 2,400 metres. Comfortable shoes matter more than anything else on this list.
